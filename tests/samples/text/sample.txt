Plain text sample

This is a plain text file. Source Lens shows it as it is, with line numbers and no colors.
It can hold notes, readme text, or any text that has no special syntax.

  - Indented lines stay indented.
  - Tabs and spaces are shown as written.

Long lines are cut at the window edge unless "Wrap lines" is on: Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua.

Unicode text is fine too: 한국어, 日本語, 中文, Français, Español.
